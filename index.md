<section class="section section-muted"><div class="container"><div class="section-head"><p class="eyebrow">A different way to think about nutrition</p><h2>Nutrition is more than instructions about food.</h2><p class="lead">Most people already know they are “supposed” to eat well. Many have also learned that hunger is a failure, rest must be earned, or their body needs constant correction. The missing piece is understanding what the body is responding to—and locating responsibility accurately across biology, learning, relationships, healthcare, resources, and environment.</p></div><div class="three-grid"><article class="card"><span class="number">1</span><h3>Understand your biology</h3><p>Learn how metabolism, hunger, stress, sleep, hormones, and development shape what you feel and do.</p></article><article class="card"><span class="number">2</span><h3>Examine food and body beliefs</h3><p>Separate useful evidence from inherited rules, diet culture, marketing, oversimplified health advice, and confident misinformation.</p></article><article class="card"><span class="number">3</span><h3>Build workable conditions</h3><p>Create routines and supports that make nourishing yourself easier in your actual life—not an imaginary perfect one.</p></article></div></div></section>
<section class="section"><div class="container"><div class="section-head"><p class="eyebrow">The Body Connection practice</p><h2>Notice. Interpret. Contextualize. Locate responsibility. Respond.</h2><p class="lead">This is an inquiry process, not another set of rules. You can enter it through one useful question and move back and forth among the steps as understanding changes.</p></div><div class="three-grid"><article class="card"><span class="number">1</span><h3>Notice</h3><p>What am I experiencing—in my body, attention, emotions, or behavior?</p></article><article class="card"><span class="number">2</span><h3>Interpret</h3><p>What might this mean? A signal is information, not an infallible command or a character verdict.</p></article><article class="card"><span class="number">3</span><h3>Contextualize</h3><p>What biology, history, learning, relationships, resources, and environments are participating?</p></article><article class="card"><span class="number">4</span><h3>Locate responsibility</h3><p>What is mine to influence, what requires support, and what belongs to a system or condition beyond individual control?</p></article><article class="card"><span class="number">5</span><h3>Respond</h3><p>What realistic next step fits the evidence, my goals, safety, access, and current capacity?</p></article></div><p style="margin-top:1.5rem"><a class="button button-secondary" href="/start-here/">Experience the practice →</a></p></div></section>
