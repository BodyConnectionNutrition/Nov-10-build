What was it like working together?

I’d like to hear what helped, what could have been better, and what you wish we’d spent more time on. Sharing feedback is optional and won’t affect your care.

If you’d like a starting point, try one of these prompts.

  • What, if anything, felt useful in our work together?
  • How well did our conversations fit the realities of your day?
  • What could I have done differently to better support you?

This draft stays on this page until you copy it. Please leave out names, diagnoses, and other identifying details.

Send your feedback privately

Paste your review into the secure messaging channel you normally use to contact me about your care. You can also write it directly there. If we work together through Nourish, use the messaging option in your Nourish account if available.

I’ll treat what you send as private feedback. If I’d like to quote any part of it publicly, I’ll contact you separately about permission.
