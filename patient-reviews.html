What did you love about working together?

If our work together made a difference in your life, I’d love to hear your story in your own words. Your experience can help someone who’s wondering what it would be like to work with me. Sharing is entirely optional.

If you’d like a starting point, try one of these prompts.

  • Was there a moment in our work together when something clicked?
  • What feels different in your day-to-day life because of our work together?
  • What would you tell someone who’s thinking about working with me?

This draft stays on this page until you copy it. Please leave out names, diagnoses, and other identifying details.

Send your testimonial

Paste your testimonial into the secure messaging channel you normally use to contact me about your care. You can also write it directly there. If we work together through Nourish, use the messaging option in your Nourish account if available.

If I’d like to feature your testimonial on the website, I’ll ask your permission and confirm the exact wording and how you’d like to be credited.
