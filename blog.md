<section class="practice">
  <p class="stage-label">The practice running through every pathway</p>
  <h2>Notice. Interpret. Contextualize. Locate responsibility. Respond.</h2>
  <p>This is an inquiry process, not another behavior standard. Move back and forth as your understanding changes.</p>
  <div class="practice-steps">
    <div class="practice-step"><b>Notice</b><small>What am I experiencing in my body, attention, emotion or behavior?</small></div>
    <div class="practice-step"><b>Interpret</b><small>What might this mean—without turning the signal into a verdict?</small></div>
    <div class="practice-step"><b>Contextualize</b><small>What biology, history, relationships, resources and environments are participating?</small></div>
    <div class="practice-step"><b>Locate responsibility</b><small>What is mine to influence, what requires support and what belongs upstream?</small></div>
    <div class="practice-step"><b>Respond</b><small>What realistic next step fits the evidence, my goals, access, safety and capacity?</small></div>
  </div>
</section>
