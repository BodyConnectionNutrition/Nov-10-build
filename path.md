## The method

Body Connection Nutrition is not a collection of unrelated worksheets. Every experience belongs to one of three kinds of work.

<div class="method-grid">
  <article class="method-card"><p class="post-meta">Stage 1</p><h3>Notice</h3><p>Make visible what usually happens automatically: a body signal, interpretation, rule, claim, cue, urge, or repeated choice.</p></article>
  <article class="method-card"><p class="post-meta">Stage 2</p><h3>Examine</h3><p>Ask what shaped it. Restore biology, learning history, evidence, reward, relationships, culture, access, resources, and environment.</p></article>
  <article class="method-card"><p class="post-meta">Stage 3</p><h3>Rebuild</h3><p>Retain what still fits, revise what does not, and create frameworks and conditions that make more room for your body, values, and actual life.</p></article>
</div>
