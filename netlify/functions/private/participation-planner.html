Participation is not mastery.

Build a response that is meaningful within the conditions you are actually in—not the conditions you are told you should be able to overcome.

Agency means meaningful participation within conditions. It does not mean total control, perfect follow-through, or personal responsibility for everything affecting you.

Before you begin

This planner moves through Notice, Interpret, Contextualize, Locate responsibility, and Respond. A response may include support, accommodation, a boundary, environmental change, collective action, waiting, or no action yet.

Your responses save only in this browser. Nothing is graded, and every plan remains revisable.

Select any conditions that matter. These are not excuses; they are part of the causal picture.

4 · What matters

What do you want the response to make room for?

Values are directions, not tests. A value can matter even when circumstances limit how fully it can be expressed.

5 · Locate responsibility

Whose participation does this situation require?

6 · Generate possibilities

What forms could participation take?

Choose every path worth considering. Selecting one does not obligate you to use it.

7 · Examine fit

What would each possibility require and protect?

Not all imaginable options are genuinely available. A workable plan accounts for this instead of treating access as willpower.

8 · Respond

Choose, wait, or leave the question open.

A response is not required. Choose the position that fits now; you may change it later.
