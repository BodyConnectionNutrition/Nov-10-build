My Food & Body Framework

Bring together what you have learned about the forces shaping food and body-related decisions—and create a visual framework you can continue revising as you learn.

This is not a final answer about you. A framework is useful because it organizes what you currently know. It should remain open to new information.

The goal is not to replace old rules with better rules. It is to see more of the system so more thoughtful responses become possible.

1 · How to use this tool

Integration is different from starting over.

If you have completed other Body Connection tools, use the patterns and insights you want to carry forward. You do not need to copy every answer. If you are using this as a standalone tool, you can still build the framework from what you already know about your experience.

The map will organize information across several domains. Some may be central for you now. Others may have little influence. Some may be unclear. Uncertainty belongs on the map too.

The map asks what participates in your food/body decisions. It does not rank those influences as good or bad and it does not diagnose you.
