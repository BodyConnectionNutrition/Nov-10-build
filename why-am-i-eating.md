## What you will do

Choose one real eating moment—a meal, snack, craving, grazing episode, or a time you kept eating after feeling full—and use it as a case study.

Instead of asking, **“What is wrong with me?”**, the experience helps you ask a more useful question:

> **What forces were converging on this decision?**

## Your Eating Drivers Map

At the end, the tool turns your answers into an **Eating Drivers Map**. The purpose is not to identify one “real” cause. Human behavior is usually multiply determined.

You might discover that one moment was shaped mostly by biological hunger and environment, while another was shaped more by restriction, reward, habit, or social context.

That is why this product includes **ongoing access and repeat use**. The value grows when you use it with different eating moments and begin to see patterns across situations.

## This is serious educational work

The method behind this experience is critical reflection: identifying the assumptions, conditions, and frameworks shaping a behavior, examining them, and separating what is happening from the judgment we automatically attach to it.

This is **education, not psychotherapy**. The tool does not diagnose an eating disorder, determine nutritional adequacy, or tell you whether you should or should not have eaten.
