Body Connection guided learning experience

Who Taught You to Eat?

This experience maps the people, routines, environments, traditions, institutions, and repeated experiences that shaped how food feels and how everyday eating decisions are made.

You are not looking for one cause. Each section explores a different kind of food learning. Select experiences that actually fit your life, then rate how much that domain influences your eating today.

1 · Early home and caregiving

This section is about the practical world of eating in childhood: who decided when food happened, how hunger and fullness were handled, how foods were offered, what meals felt like, and what resources were available.

Select experiences you personally remember having, seeing, or feeling in your childhood home.

How strongly does early home learning influence your eating today?

2 · Family, culture, and identity

This is different from the previous page. Here we are exploring what food meant: belonging, hospitality, tradition, celebration, religion, identity, family roles, and what counted as respectful or appropriate eating.

Select meanings and traditions that were part of your own food world.

How strongly do family and cultural meanings influence your eating today?

3 · Peers, school, and adolescence

As the social world expands, eating becomes more public. School, friends, sports, health education, media, and comparison can introduce new ideas about what people eat, what is acceptable, and how food connects to identity.

Select experiences from school years or adolescence that affected your relationship with food.

How strongly do peer and school experiences influence your eating today?

4 · Healthcare, nutrition, fitness, and wellness

This section explores sources that presented themselves as authorities about eating. Advice can come through healthcare, nutrition education, fitness spaces, programs, apps, books, or wellness systems.

Select the sources that have materially shaped how you evaluate food or eating.

How strongly do formal health and wellness systems influence your eating today?

5 · Media and commercial culture

Food learning also happens through repeated exposure. Advertising, social media, algorithms, television, packaging, restaurant culture, and commercial messages can shape what feels normal, desirable, convenient, responsible, or excessive.

Select the media and commercial influences that are part of your food environment.

How strongly do media and commercial messages influence your eating today?

6 · Adult life and material reality

This section is about the conditions of daily life rather than beliefs alone. Work, money, caregiving, relationships, health, access, time, transportation, cooking facilities, and household responsibilities all shape what eating actually looks like.

Select the conditions that meaningfully shape your day-to-day food decisions now.

How strongly do present-day material conditions influence your eating?

Your Food Influence Map

The size of an influence is represented by the strength rating you gave it. The selected experiences inside each domain show what contributed to that influence.

MY DAY-TO-DAY FOOD DECISIONS
This is a map of influence, not blame. Some influences may feel supportive, some constraining, and many mixed. The purpose is to see the learning system around everyday eating more clearly.
