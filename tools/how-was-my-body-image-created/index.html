A Body Connection influence-mapping experience

How was my body image created?

Map the people, settings, institutions, culture, media, and lived experiences that shaped how you see and evaluate your body.

This is not a score of whether your body image is good or bad. The goal is to make the sources of influence visible and compare their relative weight in your life today.

Influence 1 · Early home & family

What did your first environment communicate about bodies?

Think about your childhood home and caregivers. Select experiences you remember having, seeing, or feeling that shaped how bodies were understood or evaluated.

How strongly does early family influence your body image today?

Influence 2 · Peers, school & adolescence

When the body became social.

This stage adds comparison, belonging, visibility, puberty, friendship, dating, sports, teasing, praise, and public evaluation.

How strongly do peer and adolescent experiences influence your body image today?

Influence 3 · Culture, identity & belonging

Which bodies were treated as normal, desirable, respectable, or successful?

Culture shapes body meanings through race, ethnicity, gender, sexuality, religion, class, region, generation, and community expectations.

How strongly do culture and identity influence your body image today?

Influence 4 · Media & commercial culture

Which bodies were repeatedly shown, rewarded, changed, and sold?

Think about television, magazines, advertising, fashion, social media, filters, influencers, fitness content, and algorithms.

How strongly do media and commercial culture influence your body image today?

Influence 5 · Healthcare, fitness & authority

What did formal authority teach you about your body?

Doctors, coaches, trainers, health education, fitness spaces, measurements, and weight-focused systems can shape how a body is interpreted. This overlaps with food and restriction, but here the focus is specifically on body meaning.

How strongly do healthcare, fitness, and authority influence your body image today?

Influence 6 · Adult life & the lived body

What has living in a changing body taught you?

Adult life adds relationships, work, parenting, aging, health changes, clothing, movement, capability, sexuality, caregiving, and changing priorities.

How strongly does adult lived experience influence your body image today?

The picture is the point.

Body image can feel entirely personal because it is experienced inside your own mind and body. But the standards, comparisons, meanings, and expectations used to evaluate the body are learned in relationship with a much larger world.

This map lets you see which parts of that world have had the most influence—and where your own lived experience belongs in the picture.
