What the tool helps you do

1 · Identify

Find the words that feel alive

Choose values through recognition, relief, energy, longing, or grief—not through qualities you think you should perform.

2 · Define

Make the words yours

Translate each value into your own meaning instead of accepting someone else's expectations.

3 · Deconstruct

Notice inherited rules

Identify what taught a food or body rule, what it promised to protect, and what following it has cost.

4 · Locate

Map the conflict

Place outside pressure, body information, circumstances, and the value you want more room for into the same view.

5 · Expand

Create multiple possibilities

Test whether a value is expanding your options—or quietly becoming another rigid rule.

6 · Experiment

Choose one flexible action

Try one values-aligned action, observe without grading it, and revise using what you learn.
