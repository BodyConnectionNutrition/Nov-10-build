The Cope Bank

You have already learned things that help. Keep them somewhere you can find them again when stress, exhaustion, or overwhelm makes remembering difficult.

You do not have to invent care in the middle of a difficult moment. Strategies can include rest, asking for help, reducing demands, changing an environment, practical accommodations, or choosing not to act right now. Not everything works every time.

Entries are stored in this browser on this device, not in an account. Clearing browser data or using private browsing can erase them. Export a backup to keep a copy. Avoid entering information you would not want stored on this device.

My saved strategies

The Cope Bank is a personal reflection and support-planning tool, not emergency or crisis care. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. In the U.S., call or text 988 for crisis support. You can also use this tool alongside care from a qualified professional.
