The shared method

Notice → Interpret → Contextualize → Respond → Locate responsibility

1 · NoticeWhat is happening?2 · InterpretWhat meaning arrived?3 · ContextualizeWhat shaped this?4 · RespondWhat, if anything, fits now?5 · Locate responsibilityWhat belongs where?

These are moves within inquiry, not levels to pass. A tool appears under the move where it is most useful, while the labels on each card show the other parts of the method it supports.

Agency means meaningful participation within conditions. It is not total control, perfect follow-through, or responsibility for everything affecting you. Participation may include action, support, accommodation, a boundary, environmental change, waiting, or no action yet.
