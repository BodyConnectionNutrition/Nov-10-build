The Recipe Box

You already know meals that work for you. Save them here so you don't have to reinvent dinner, lunch, or breakfast every time you're hungry, tired, or overwhelmed.

There is no ideal meal to earn and no required plan to follow. Start with one familiar meal. Frozen, prepared, assembled, and takeout meals count.

Your meals are saved in this browser on this device, not to an account. Clearing browser data or using private browsing may erase them. Export a backup if you want to keep them.
